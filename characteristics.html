Table 8. Product characteristics and preliminary targets. "Type" follows the brief's distinction: a requirement must be met, an objective is maximised or minimised, a constraint bounds the design absolutely.

Confidence in the targets

Six of the thirteen targets are supported by survey data or competitor benchmarking. Five rest on team judgement and are labelled accordingly. The carrying-capacity, tracking and durability numbers have no customer evidence behind them yet and are the weakest of the set.

Primary drivers of product success

Customer value comes from the two outcome specifications — malfunction reduction and user confidence. Market differentiation comes from price and weight, the two axes on which every existing instrument is disqualified as a consumer product. Technical feasibility will be decided by whether the no-damage constraint (≥ 95% of tested equipment unaffected) can be met at a $100 price and 7.5 kg mass.
